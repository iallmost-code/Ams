# Combat and progression design

The intended presentation uses strong directional attack silhouettes, layered hit impacts, compact touch controls and clearly visible character growth. All names, artwork and animation choreography in AetherStory are original.

Characters earn five assignable attribute points and two skill points per level. The main-to-secondary attack contribution is 4:1. Each attribute also improves supporting combat statistics, and mastery improves attack, skill power and the minimum damage roll. These coefficients preserve the existing world difficulty while giving players meaningful build choices.

Primary attacks use a hand-painted twenty-frame atlas. The cleaned source keeps equal-sized cells and transparent borders, while the shipped WebP preserves alpha with a smaller download. The full-power pose is held long enough to read during live phone play. Skill effects render after both the player and companion.
